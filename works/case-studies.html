How to interpret the results

Google Search Console and Google Analytics report first-party site data when correctly configured. SEMrush and Ahrefs provide third-party estimates and audit indicators. Search-result screenshots can vary by location, device, personalization and date. Metrics should always be shown with their source and reporting period.
