On-page SEO quality checklist

Relevance

  • Target keyword and search intent match
  • Unique title and meta description
  • Clear H1 and logical subheadings
  • Helpful, original page content

Discoverability

  • Contextual internal links
  • Descriptive image alt text
  • Readable URL structure
  • Relevant structured data where appropriate
