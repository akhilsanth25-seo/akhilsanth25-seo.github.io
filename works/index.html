HOW TO READ THIS PORTFOLIO

Evidence first. Context always.

Screenshots are included as dated reporting or audit evidence. Third-party SEO estimates, Google Search Console metrics and analytics data are identified separately. A screenshot alone does not establish that a specific optimization caused a change in performance.

Reporting source

Each evidence image identifies the platform or type of report where it can be recognized.

Reporting period

Dates in screenshot filenames and report views help preserve the original measurement context.

Role transparency

Project summaries describe the available work and evidence without claiming unsupported individual attribution.
