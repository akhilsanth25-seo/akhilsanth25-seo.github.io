ORGANIC SEARCH ANALYTICS

Google Analytics & Search Console Performance

Explore Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console reporting evidence, including organic traffic, clicks, impressions, search queries and website engagement.

What I analyze

I use first-party analytics and search performance reports to understand how visitors find a website, which pages appear in search and how organic performance changes over time.

Google Search Console

Clicks, impressions, CTR, average position, search queries and page-level search visibility.

Google Analytics 4

Active users, new users, acquisition channels, engaged sessions and landing-page activity.
