OFF-PAGE SEO

Backlink Analysis, Referring Domains & Competitor Research

Explore off-page SEO evidence covering backlink profiles, referring domains, competitor research and third-party authority metrics.

Off-page SEO analysis

Off-page analysis helps evaluate the external link profile and competitive landscape of a website. Third-party metrics are useful for comparison, but they are not Google ranking scores.

Backlink profile

Review referring pages, backlink attributes, anchor text and new or lost links.

Referring domains

Assess linking-domain patterns and compare profiles across competing websites.

Competitor research

Identify link opportunities and understand differences in competitor backlink profiles.
